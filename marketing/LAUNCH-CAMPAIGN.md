# MetaSvit AI transformation launch

Prepared 31 October 2026. Social content is drafted, not posted or scheduled. Website publishing and campaign publishing are separate actions. Owner: founder, with the delivery lead supplying technical examples.

## Message and audience

Core message: **Build an AI-powered business with senior engineers working inside your team.**

Speak to European business and technology leaders who have AI experiments, manual handoffs or fragmented internal systems. Start with a process they recognise; explain forward-deployed engineering as the method. Support the offer with individual senior experience and the existing engineering portfolio. Offer a conversation about one workflow.

LinkedIn is the primary channel hypothesis for enterprise buyers. Use the founder's account for the first-person launch and technical lessons, the company page for service updates, and X for concise engineering observations. No outreach, account change, ad spend or posting is authorised by this draft pack.

## Lead path

Post → relevant service/delivery page → workflow brief → fit conversation → scoped paid discovery → build decision. Ask for the workflow, systems involved, owner, rough volume, constraints and desired improvement. Do not request confidential datasets in a public comment.

Use query parameters on actual links, for example `?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=ai_transformation_launch&utm_content=founder_announcement`. Canonicals remain the clean URL. No new tracking pixels are needed for the launch.

## Measure

Record a launch-date Search Console snapshot and compare complete reporting windows later. Track post impressions and clicks from the selected platform, then privately count qualified enquiries, fit conversations, discovery proposals and paid engagements. Visits, clicks and impressions are different measures. The contact API's confirmed delivery is the conversion boundary; do not count an email-link click as an enquiry.

Initial learning goals: five useful buyer conversations and a clearer ranking of the first three workflow problems. The strategy's paid-discovery targets are hypotheses. Do not invent traffic or conversion baselines. Visitor analytics remains a separate provider decision; this campaign does not activate paid analytics or advertising.

## Voice and content rules

Direct, practical and specific. Show the work: a workflow map, permission boundary, evaluation result or operating decision. Use “AI transformation” as the category and explain “forward-deployed engineering” on first mention. Describe what is offered today. Forecasts belong in sourced commentary, not promises of capability, delivery dates or guaranteed return.

Review public copy for attribution: Amazon and Miro belong to individual team experience, while the work page contains MetaSvit's actual projects. No vendor/client logos or named client outcome without an established relationship and permission.
