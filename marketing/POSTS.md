## 3. Founder LinkedIn: choose the first workflow

A useful first AI project starts with a task you can describe clearly.

Who does it? How often? Which systems are involved? Where does it wait for someone? What would an acceptable result look like?

Then ask whether the data, permissions and operational owner are available. A repeated process with clear boundaries gives a team something it can build and measure.

For AI transformation at MetaSvit, workflow discovery is the first engagement. The output is a business case and a build decision, with the constraints visible.

## 6. Founder LinkedIn: measure the pilot

Before building an AI workflow, write down how you will judge it.

Measure time per task, accepted outputs, corrections, unresolved exceptions and cost per completed outcome. Include the human review and operating costs.

After rollout, check whether people actually use it and whether the improvement holds on real work.

That evidence is what should decide whether to expand the deployment.

At MetaSvit, this baseline is part of discovery, and the evaluation stays with the workflow as it changes.
